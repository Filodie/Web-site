Picture books for talking about difficult feelings

A story lets you talk about hard things without putting the child on the spot. The character lives the feeling for them, and the child can recognize themselves at their own pace.

Each Filodie picture book tells a gentle story for ages 4 to 8, followed by two pages to do with the child and a guide for adults: what to say, what to avoid and when to ask for help.
