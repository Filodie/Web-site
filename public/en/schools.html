For schools

Tools designed by a special care counsellor (SCC) for everyday school life: routines, transitions, pictograms and support for students who need a little more. Your whole team can use them, with a single invoice in the school’s name.

What your team gets

  • Ready-to-print PDFs, fillable on screen, in English and French.
  • An editable Word version for most toolkits, to adapt each tool to the student and your setting.
  • Keychain pictograms and posters designed for Québec schools.
  • Tools that support intervention, without replacing assessment or the work of the school’s other professionals.

How to order

  1. Email me with the products you want, the school’s name and address, and a contact person.

  3. As soon as payment is received, you receive the files by email.
