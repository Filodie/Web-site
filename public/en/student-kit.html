What’s inside the SCC student kit

Sixteen tools designed for special care counselling students: to arrive at your internship prepared, write solid assignments and bridge the gap to your first job. Everything comes as PDFs to print or fill in on screen, in French and English.

For internships

  • Starting My Internship Well: prepare before you arrive, get to know the setting and the clientele, agree on expectations with your supervisor, review your first week.
  • My Reflective Internship Journal: goals, significant situations, emotions and values, feedback, mid-term and final reviews.
  • Ethics and Boundaries in Internships: boundaries in the relationship, confidentiality, social media, working through a dilemma.
  • Planning an Educational Activity: objective, timed plan, materials, the unexpected, evaluation.

For coursework and intervention

  • Building an Intervention Plan: strengths and needs, observable goals, strategies, review.
  • Writing a Progress Note: facts versus interpretations, the right words, structure, examples.
  • Using the HDM-DCP Model: personal factors, environment, life habits.
  • Analyzing a Group: structure, dynamics and roles, a case scenario with answers.
  • Presenting a Case Study: profile, needs analysis, hypotheses, presentation.
  • Listening to Help: open questions, reflecting, paraphrasing, self-assessing an interview.

For working with others

  • Working with Families: first contact, delivering a difficult message, parent meetings.
  • Working on an Interdisciplinary Team: professional roles, meetings, constructive disagreement.
  • Intervening in Diverse Contexts: intercultural stance, culture shock, adapting your intervention.
  • My Resource Network: public services, community organizations, helplines, referring a person.

For succeeding in school and beyond

  • Studying Effectively: planning the semester, retrieval practice, spaced study, exam preparation.
  • Landing My First SCC Job: portfolio, résumé, interview, job search.
