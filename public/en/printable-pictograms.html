Shop / Printable pictograms

Printable pictograms

A clear picture is often better than a long instruction. These pictograms are printed, cut out and laminated, then slipped onto a ring on a belt loop so they are always at hand, in class or on an outing.

Each keychain kit has its own colour: purple for emotions, blue for needs and requests, green for expected behaviours, red for what not to do, orange for routines and daily moments, turquoise for calming strategies, pink for showing where it hurts, yellow for school life in Québec (winter, school daycare, PD days), brown for recess and play, navy blue for meals and cooking, burgundy for health and care, olive green for community outings and forest green for the holiday season. The characters are an original Filodie creation: eleven children in colour (a girl, a boy, a girl with glasses, a boy with glasses, a boy with hearing aids, a girl with hearing aids, a girl with noise-cancelling headphones, a girl with a cochlear implant, a boy with dental braces and, except for emotions, a boy who uses a wheelchair and a boy with a leg brace), plus a girl and a boy in black and white. All thirteen kits are also available together as a bundle.
