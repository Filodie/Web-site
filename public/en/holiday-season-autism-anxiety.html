Getting through the holidays with an autistic or anxious child

No routine, lots of people, noise, surprises: the holiday break can be exhausting for an autistic, ADHD or anxious child, and for their whole family.

You will find a toolkit for planning the break day by day, preparing each visit and responding when it all becomes too much, holiday keychain pictograms and calming tools. Not every family celebrates Christmas: the tools adapt to every celebration.
