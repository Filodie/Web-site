Behaviour and crisis tools

Behind challenging behaviour there is often a need. These tools help you observe, understand what triggers a crisis and respond in a calm, consistent way.

You will find observation tools, prevention plans, positive reinforcement by age, a ready-to-use training and workbooks to do with the child to learn how to calm down.
